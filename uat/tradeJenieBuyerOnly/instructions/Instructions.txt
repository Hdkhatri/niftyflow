============================KEY STRUCTURE===============================================

<USER><UNDERLYING><STRAT><MODE>-<HEDGE><HEDGE_ROLLOVER_TYPE><TF><EXP><EXPD>
<USER> UNIQUENAME OF USER  
<UNDERLYING> NIFTY / SENSEX / NIFTYBANK
<STRAT>	GOD / RLY / HD	GOD
<TF>	T03, T05, T15, T30, H01, H02, D01	T05
<EXP>	W or M	W
<MODE> H NH
<HEDGE> = P10, M100, M200
<HEDGE_ROLLOVER_TYPE> SEMI(S) / FULL(F)
example:
HDK_NIFTY_RLY_NH_H01_W
HDK_SENSEX_RLY_H-P10_S_H01_W
