Retirement Planner

Plan for a secure retirement. Estimate your retirement corpus, monthly income, and inflation-adjusted expenses. Enter your details to get started.

What is a Retirement Planner?

A retirement planner helps you estimate the amount you need to retire comfortably, considering inflation, expenses, and investment returns.

How to use the Retirement Planner?

  • Enter your current age, retirement age, and current savings.
  • Enter your monthly investment, expected return, and expected monthly expense in retirement.
  • Enter expected years in retirement and inflation rate.
  • Click Calculate Retirement to see your results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the retirement corpus calculated?

The corpus is calculated based on your savings, investments, expected returns, and inflation-adjusted expenses during retirement.

Why consider inflation?

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of your money over time. Planning for inflation ensures your retirement income is sufficient.
