What is a Stock Average Price Calculator?

This calculator helps you find the average buy price after multiple stock purchases at different prices.

How to use the Stock Average Price Calculator?

  • Enter quantity and price for each purchase. Add more rows as needed.
  • Click Calculate to see your average price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is average price calculated?

Average price = (Total amount invested) / (Total quantity bought)

Why is average price important?

It helps you know your break-even point after averaging down or up.
