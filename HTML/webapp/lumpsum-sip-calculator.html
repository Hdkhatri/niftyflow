What is a Lumpsum + SIP Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate the combined future value of a lumpsum and monthly SIP investment.

How to use the Lumpsum + SIP Calculator?

  • Enter your lumpsum, SIP, years, and expected return.
  • Click Calculate to see your total future value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the combined value calculated?

The calculator adds the future value of the lumpsum and SIP investments, both compounding at the same rate.

Can I use this for mutual funds?

Yes, this is ideal for mutual fund investors combining one-time and monthly investments.
