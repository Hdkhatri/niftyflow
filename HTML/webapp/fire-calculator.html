What is a FIRE Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate your FIRE number (required corpus) and how many years it will take to reach it.

How to use the FIRE Calculator?

  • Enter your monthly expenses, savings, expected return, and withdrawal rate.
  • Click Calculate to see your FIRE number and years to FIRE.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the FIRE number calculated?

FIRE number = Annual Expenses / Withdrawal Rate

What is a safe withdrawal rate?

4% is commonly used, but you can adjust it based on your risk tolerance.
