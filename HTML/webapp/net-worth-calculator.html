Net Worth Calculator

Calculate your net worth by entering your total assets and liabilities. Get a clear picture of your financial health.

What is Net Worth?

Net worth is the difference between your total assets and total liabilities. It is a key indicator of your financial health.

How to use the Net Worth Calculator?

  • Enter your total assets (cash, investments, property, etc.).
  • Enter your total liabilities (loans, credit card debt, etc.).
  • Click Calculate Net Worth to see your result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is net worth important?

Tracking your net worth helps you understand your financial progress and make informed decisions about saving, investing, and spending.

What counts as an asset or liability?

Assets include cash, investments, property, and valuables. Liabilities include all debts and obligations.
