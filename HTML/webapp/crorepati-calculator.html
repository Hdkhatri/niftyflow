What is a Crorepati Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate how long it will take to reach your target wealth (e.g., ₹1 crore) with regular SIP investments and a given return rate.

How to use the Crorepati Calculator?

  • Enter your target amount, monthly SIP, and expected annual return.
  • Click Calculate Crorepati to see your result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the time to target calculated?

The calculator uses the SIP future value formula and iterates to find the number of months needed to reach your target.

Can I change the target?

Yes, you can set any target amount, not just ₹1 crore.
