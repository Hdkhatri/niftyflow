What is an Options Profit/Loss Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate the profit or loss for your options trades based on trade details and expiry price.

How to use the Options P&L Calculator?

  • Select option type (Call/Put), Buy/Sell, and enter trade details.
  • Click Calculate to see your profit or loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is options P&L calculated?

Profit or loss is calculated based on the difference between spot price at expiry, strike price, premium, and lot size.

What is the maximum loss in options?

For buyers, the maximum loss is the premium paid. For sellers, the risk can be unlimited (for naked options).
