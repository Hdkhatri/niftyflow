What is an RD Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate the maturity value of your recurring deposit, considering monthly deposits and compounding interest.

How to use the RD Calculator?

  • Enter your monthly deposit, tenure, and interest rate.
  • Click Calculate to see your maturity value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is RD maturity calculated?

The calculator uses the future value formula for recurring deposits with compounding interest.

Is RD safe?

RDs are considered safe and are offered by banks and post offices in India.
