What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) calculator helps you estimate the future value of your regular mutual fund investments. It shows how much wealth you can accumulate by investing a fixed amount every month over a period of time at an expected rate of return.

How to use the SIP Calculator?

  • Enter your monthly SIP amount.
  • Enter the expected annual return rate (in %).
  • Enter the number of years you plan to invest.
  • Click Calculate to see the results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is SIP maturity amount calculated?

The SIP maturity amount is calculated using the future value formula for a recurring investment, considering compounding returns.

Is SIP better than lumpsum?

SIP helps average out market volatility and is suitable for regular savers. Lumpsum is suitable for those with a large amount to invest at once.
