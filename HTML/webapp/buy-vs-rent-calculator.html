Compare the total outflow for buying vs renting a home. Enter your details to get started.

What is a Buy vs Rent Calculator?

This calculator helps you compare the total cost of buying a home versus renting over a period of time.

How to use the Buy vs Rent Calculator?

  • Enter home price, down payment, loan details, and rent info.
  • Click Calculate to see the total outflow for both options.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the comparison done?

The calculator estimates total EMI outflow for buying and total rent paid for renting, including rent escalation.

What factors should I consider?

Consider opportunity cost, property appreciation, and your personal situation in addition to the numbers.
