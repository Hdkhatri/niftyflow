What is a Retirement Planner Calculator?

This calculator helps you estimate the retirement corpus you need and the monthly savings required to reach your goal.

How to use the Retirement Planner Calculator?

  • Enter your age, retirement age, expenses, expected return, inflation, and current savings.
  • Click Calculate to see your required corpus and monthly savings needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the retirement corpus calculated?

It estimates future expenses adjusted for inflation and calculates the corpus needed to sustain those expenses post-retirement.

Why consider inflation?

Inflation increases your expenses over time, so your retirement goal must account for it.
