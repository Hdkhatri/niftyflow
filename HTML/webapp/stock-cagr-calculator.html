What is a Stock CAGR Calculator?

This calculator helps you find the compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for your stock or investment over a period of time.

How to use the Stock CAGR Calculator?

  • Enter your initial value, final value, and investment period in years.
  • Click Calculate to see your CAGR.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is CAGR calculated?

CAGR = (Final Value / Initial Value)1/Years - 1

Why is CAGR important?

CAGR shows the annual growth rate of an investment, smoothing out volatility.
