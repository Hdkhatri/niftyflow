Position Sizing Calculator

Find the optimal position size for your trades based on your risk tolerance and stop loss.

What is a Position Sizing Calculator?

This calculator helps you determine the optimal number of shares or lots to buy or sell in a trade, based on your risk tolerance and stop loss.

How to use the Position Sizing Calculator?

  • Enter your account capital, risk per trade, entry price, and stop loss price.
  • Click Calculate to see your optimal position size.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is position size calculated?

Position size = (Account Capital × Risk %) / (Entry Price − Stop Loss Price)

Why is position sizing important?

Proper position sizing helps manage risk and avoid large losses in trading.
