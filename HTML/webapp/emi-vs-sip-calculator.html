EMI vs SIP Calculator

Compare your loan EMI outgo with the potential growth of a SIP investment. Enter your loan and SIP details to see which is better for your goals.

What is EMI vs SIP?

This calculator helps you compare the total outgo on a loan (EMI) versus the potential growth of investing the same amount in a SIP.

How to use the EMI vs SIP Calculator?

  • Enter your loan amount, tenure, and interest rate.
  • Enter your monthly SIP and expected return rate.
  • Click Compare to see the results.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the result show?

The result shows the total amount paid as EMI and the future value of the same amount invested as SIP.

Which is better?

It depends on your financial goals, risk appetite, and loan/investment rates.
